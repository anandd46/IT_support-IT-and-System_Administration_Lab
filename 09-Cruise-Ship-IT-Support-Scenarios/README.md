# Module 09 — Cruise Ship IT Support Scenarios

This module contains nine simulated IT support scenarios designed to demonstrate practical IT support skills relevant to onboard environments such as the Explora Journeys IT Systems Assistant role.

> **Important Disclaimer:** These scenarios are simulated exercises based on general IT support principles. They are entirely fictional and educational. They do not represent Explora Journeys' actual onboard network architecture, proprietary systems, or operational procedures. No confidential, proprietary, or insider information about any shipping company or vessel is included.

## Shipboard IT Support Considerations

Working as an IT Systems Assistant on a cruise ship involves unique challenges compared to a typical office environment:

- **Limited internet connectivity:** Satellite bandwidth is limited and expensive. Troubleshooting may need to be done offline.
- **No external vendor support on demand:** You cannot easily call a technician to come on-site. Self-sufficiency is critical.
- **Change-control procedures:** Any changes to critical shipboard systems (navigation, communications, safety) must follow strict authorisation procedures.
- **24/7 operations:** The ship never stops. IT issues at any hour need attention.
- **Multiple departments:** Bridge, Engineering, Housekeeping, Food & Beverage, Guest Services all have IT needs.
- **Escalation path:** Know when to escalate to the IT Officer or shore-based support team.
